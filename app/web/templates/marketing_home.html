How it works

From a throwaway message to a decision of record.

1

Watch the conversation

Connect your GitHub repo and the Discord channels where your team actually argues. ArborDocs reads new messages — it never posts.

2

Extract & reconcile

An LLM pipeline pulls out durable decisions and surfaces the related code and docs from your repo, so you can see what the codebase actually does.

3

Review & publish

You approve, edit, or reject each one. Approved decisions land in a searchable, read-only portal with full history and supersession chains.

Human review, always

Nothing becomes a decision of record without a person approving it. The pipeline proposes; your team decides.

Checked against the code

Every decision links to the related files in your repo by similarity, so drift between "what we said" and "what we built" is visible.

Full history & supersession

When a decision reverses an older one, the two are linked. Every event is in an append-only audit log.

Read-only for everyone else

Anyone on the team can search the portal to answer "why did we do it this way?" — without touching the record.
